# RESONATE — Golden filament / owl motion reference
Status: visual research only, **not active app work**.
Date: 24 October 2026.
Gates: issue #7 acceptance first; future issue #9 motion phase only after Chas approves.

## Chas's visual reference
The closing golden-owl clip he recorded from Truth Well Told Tarot on 24 October 2026 conveys the **movement**, not simply the look. The owl and the thin golden filament should move at the same type of pace: smooth, deliberate, elegant, restrained and cohesive. His words in issue #9: the golden thread should be like **'kissing the cards as they turn'**.

## Exploration constraints
- Obsidian/black and established gold RESONATE identity; preserve original gold-R-in-circle branding.
- Thin, luminous filament follows card arc and edges as each card rotates; subtle glow, not casino fireworks.
- Gentle acceleration and deceleration; match owl motion's graceful temporal rhythm. Do not invent an exact duration from a static frame; video timing must be measured on approval.
- Card selection and order decided by secure reading state; animation never influences randomness.
- Readable Rider–Waite artwork, original card orientation, position labels and maximum two clarifier layers.
- Mobile GPU, battery, reduced-motion settings, keyboard/screen-reader accessibility, motion off toggle.
- Animation can be a 5–10 second **offline study** using Blender, subject to Chas's decision. No implementation into the current reading engine or site before the acceptance gate.

## Suggested research deliverable
A visual storyboard, easing diagram, prototype options (CSS/WebGL/Lottie/video where appropriate), expected performance and licensing trade-offs. Chas selects/approves any resulting design or product change.
