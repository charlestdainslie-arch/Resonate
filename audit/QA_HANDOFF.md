## 2. Position definitions vs the implemented specification

The repository has **no separate specification** for spread positions. The definitions in `app.js` are the
only ones. `audit/POSITIONS.md` lists all 26 positions verbatim, with line numbers; it is generated by
`node audit/tools/positions.cjs`. History:

| Spread | Introduced | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship (5) | 374ba57, 9 Sep 2026 | No external source recorded. |
| Seven-card V (7) | fc6caba and 7151e34, 10 Sep 2026 | Commit message: "Apply Fenton-Smith timing to seven-card interpretations". No citation is held in the repo; the attribution is unverified. |
| Celtic Cross (10) | 374ba57, 9 Sep 2026 | Standard Celtic Cross roles; no external source recorded. |

User-facing provenance: no page, script or data file shown to users mentions Fenton-Smith (checked with
`grep -ri fenton` over the app's .js, .html and .json files). The name appears only in internal audit and
planning documents, as an unverified attribution.

Conflicts between the definitions and what is implemented (flagged for Chas, not changed):
- **Seven-card position 4, "You / Answer".** The definition says that for a question reading "this card gives
  the answer and is interpreted last". The app and Max read it fourth, in order. The position's own lens
  text tells the seeker to "return to this card last". Nothing reorders it.
- **Celtic Cross pairs** (1 & 2, 3 & 5, 4 & 6, 7 & 8, 9 & 10) are used by the new "wider spread" line. They are
  RESONATE's reading method, not part of the position definitions. Deep to confirm.
- **Fixed today (Q2b):** "Your Approach" and "Potential Outcome" were not getting their intended position
  lens, because of a case-sensitive check. See `audit/QA_ENGINE_CLAUDE_NOTES.md`.

## 3. Card IDs from the 78-card registry

Every sample stores card **IDs**. The registry is `data.js`: IDs 0–77, unique names, Majors 0–21. This is
checked by `tests/qa-samples.test.cjs`, which also checks that every sample ID resolves and that no card
appears twice in a reading. `audit/qa-samples/README.md` lists every ID used, with its registry name,
arcana and element, and the sha256 of `data.js`.

## 4. Major Arcana rule vs the two-clarifier limit

The rule as implemented: a Major gets Clarifier 1; a Major Clarifier 1 gets Clarifier 2; at most 2 clarifiers.
It is unchanged. Labelled Chas's teaching rule; not attributed to Paul Fenton-Smith without a source.

**Conflict, flagged for Chas.** If Clarifier 2 is also a Major, the rule as taught would continue, and the
limit stops it. This build states that on the page. Frequency: **7.18% exact** for one Major card; **about
19.0% (estimate)** of fully clarified Celtic Cross readings. Both are derived by
`audit/tools/limit-frequency.cjs` (formula, seed and 95% interval in `audit/RULE_RECONCILIATION.md`, which
also has designs A–D). The earlier unseeded "7.1% / 18.9%" figures are withdrawn.

## 7. Reproducing it (Deep)

Without Node (interpretation review):
1. Get the evidence ZIP from Chas. Check its SHA-256 against the Issue #49 post.
2. Unzip it, then in the folder run `python3 -m http.server 8080`. Any static web server will do.
3. Open `http://localhost:8080/presenter-uat.html?qa=B03`, or any sample ID. The page shows exactly the
   reading in `audit/qa-samples/B03.md`; tap ▶ Watch Max for the narration.
4. Compare the page with the `.md` file. To check the inputs, look up each ID in `data.js`.

With Node 22 and Playwright (re-running the evidence):
- `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`: unit tests, including the sample and registry checks.
- `node tests/reading-engine-qa.browser.cjs`: all 21 samples plus boundary and regression checks.
- `QA_SHA=<sha> QA_TESTLOG=<log> node audit/tools/qa-samples.cjs <out-dir>`: regenerates the sample files.
  Compare them with the committed ones using `diff -r`.
- `bash audit/tools/run-all-tests.sh <out-dir>`: every suite.
