## Acceptance criteria (for Deep to mark PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED with evidence)

- **orientation:** Each card is read with the meaning for its orientation (upright or reversed text from the card data below), and a reversed card is not merely the negation of its upright meaning.
- **position:** The position lens names this position and reads the card through the position’s defined meaning (see the table), without drifting in time or role.
- **neighbours:** The "wider spread" line relates the card to its pair partner (Celtic Cross) or neighbour (other spreads) without inventing a connection the data does not support.
- **clarifier1:** Clarifier 1 is read as modifying the original card ("how it modifies the original card"), naming both, not as a separate definition.
- **clarifier2:** Clarifier 2 develops the relationship between both earlier cards, naming all three.
- **synthesis:** "Together" is a synthesis of the cards, not a list; revisions are shown (Revision 1 kept readable when Clarifier 2 arrives; Revision 2 when the Clarity card arrives).
- **clarity:** Option B (approved 7 Nov): a Clarity card appears only when the original and both clarifiers are Major, is a Minor not already in the reading, and is read as grounding the three Major cards; a Minor original with two Major clarifiers gets none and the limit is stated.
- **max:** Max’s lines are the reading’s own words (quoted), in reading order; before Finish he invites and does not sign off; after Finish the closing comes once.
- **failure:** Deep’s six failure modes: templating, positional drift, reverse-as-opposite, parallel clarification, concatenation instead of synthesis, numerical/symbolic overreach.

### Card data each card was read from (data.js; provenance OPEN, see audit/sources/DATASET_NOTES.md)

- **The Lovers** (ID 6; Major; number 6; element Air; astrology Gemini)
  - Upright keywords: Alignment, Partnership, Choice. Upright: A meaningful relationship or choice asks you to act in line with your values. Mutual openness matters more than an ideal image of harmony; choose the commitments you can honestly sustain.
  - Reversed keywords: Misalignment, Division, Compromise. Reversed: Attraction, duty and personal values may be pulling in different directions. Name the disagreement before promising unity, and avoid sacrificing your voice merely to keep a connection intact.
- **Two of Cups** (ID 37; Minor, Cups; number 2, Two; element Water; astrology Water · Cups)
  - Upright keywords: Mutuality, Connection, Agreement. Upright: A connection can deepen through reciprocal attention and honest exchange. Ask what each person is offering freely and make the agreement explicit instead of relying on an imagined harmony.
  - Reversed keywords: Imbalance, Disconnection, Broken Trust. Reversed: A relationship or agreement may be unequal or out of tune. Name the difference and assess whether repair is mutual rather than assuming one person can restore the connection alone.
- **The Star** (ID 17; Major; number 17; element Air; astrology Aquarius)
  - Upright keywords: Hope, Renewal, Openness. Upright: After strain, a quieter sense of possibility can return. Replenish what has been depleted and give a modest hope some daily attention; renewal does not require pretending that the difficulty never happened.
  - Reversed keywords: Discouragement, Disconnection, Depletion. Reversed: Hope may feel remote because you are tired or comparing your recovery with an ideal. Seek a reliable source of nourishment and notice small signs of connection before demanding confidence.
- **King of Swords** (ID 63; Minor, Swords; number —, King; element Air; astrology Air · Swords)
  - Upright keywords: Reason, Integrity, Clear Standards. Upright: Bring clear standards and careful reasoning to a decision. Explain the principles you are using, consider relevant evidence and make room for the human effects of a technically correct answer.
  - Reversed keywords: Rigidity, Manipulation, Cold Authority. Reversed: Reason may be used selectively to justify control or conceal feeling. Test your standards for consistency and invite scrutiny of the assumptions behind the conclusion.
