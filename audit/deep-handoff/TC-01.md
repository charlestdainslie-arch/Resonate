## Acceptance criteria (for Deep to mark PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED with evidence)

- **orientation:** Each card is read with the meaning for its orientation (upright or reversed text from the card data below), and a reversed card is not merely the negation of its upright meaning.
- **position:** The position lens names this position and reads the card through the position’s defined meaning (see the table), without drifting in time or role.
- **neighbours:** The "wider spread" line relates the card to its pair partner (Celtic Cross) or neighbour (other spreads) without inventing a connection the data does not support.
- **clarifier1:** Clarifier 1 is read as modifying the original card ("how it modifies the original card"), naming both, not as a separate definition.
- **clarifier2:** Clarifier 2 develops the relationship between both earlier cards, naming all three.
- **synthesis:** "Together" is a synthesis of the cards, not a list; revisions are shown (Revision 1 kept readable when Clarifier 2 arrives; Revision 2 when the Clarity card arrives).
- **clarity:** Option B (approved 7 Nov): a Clarity card appears only when the original and both clarifiers are Major, is a Minor not already in the reading, and is read as grounding the three Major cards; a Minor original with two Major clarifiers gets none and the limit is stated.
- **max:** Max’s lines are the reading’s own words (quoted), in reading order; before Finish he invites and does not sign off; after Finish the closing comes once.
- **failure:** Deep’s six failure modes: templating, positional drift, reverse-as-opposite, parallel clarification, concatenation instead of synthesis, numerical/symbolic overreach.

- **Ace of Pentacles** (ID 64; Minor, Pentacles; number 1, Ace; element Earth; astrology Earth · Pentacles)
  - Upright keywords: Opportunity, Resources, Grounding. Upright: A practical opportunity can become valuable through steady attention. Look at the resources, skills or conditions available and choose a first step that makes the possibility concrete.
  - Reversed keywords: Missed Opening, Unready Plan, Instability. Reversed: An opportunity may need preparation before it can take root. Check costs, capacity and follow-through rather than confusing the promise of a result with a workable plan.
- **Five of Cups** (ID 40; Minor, Cups; number 5, Five; element Water; astrology Water · Cups)
  - Upright keywords: Grief, Disappointment, Remaining Support. Upright: Give a disappointment its proper weight without making it the whole picture. Grief and remaining support can coexist; acknowledge the loss, then notice who or what is still available.
  - Reversed keywords: Acceptance, Lingering Regret, Reconnection. Reversed: Acceptance may be growing, or regret may be repeating without bringing new understanding. Reach toward the support that remains and let moving forward include, rather than erase, the loss.
- **Six of Wands** (ID 27; Minor, Wands; number 6, Six; element Fire; astrology Fire · Wands)
  - Upright keywords: Recognition, Progress, Encouragement. Upright: An effort is being recognised or a difficult stage has been passed. Receive encouragement, share the credit and use the confidence to support continued work rather than rest everything on applause.
  - Reversed keywords: Dependence on Praise, Setback, Private Success. Reversed: Recognition may be delayed, uneven or too important to your confidence. Reconnect with the standard that matters to you and acknowledge progress that others have not noticed.
