## Acceptance criteria (for Deep to mark PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED with evidence)

- **orientation:** Each card is read with the meaning for its orientation (upright or reversed text from the card data below), and a reversed card is not merely the negation of its upright meaning.
- **position:** The position lens names this position and reads the card through the position’s defined meaning (see the table), without drifting in time or role.
- **neighbours:** The "wider spread" line relates the card to its pair partner (Celtic Cross) or neighbour (other spreads) without inventing a connection the data does not support.
- **clarifier1:** Clarifier 1 is read as modifying the original card ("how it modifies the original card"), naming both, not as a separate definition.
- **clarifier2:** Clarifier 2 develops the relationship between both earlier cards, naming all three.
- **synthesis:** "Together" is a synthesis of the cards, not a list; revisions are shown (Revision 1 kept readable when Clarifier 2 arrives; Revision 2 when the Clarity card arrives).
- **clarity:** Option B (approved 7 Nov): a Clarity card appears only when the original and both clarifiers are Major, is a Minor not already in the reading, and is read as grounding the three Major cards; a Minor original with two Major clarifiers gets none and the limit is stated.
- **max:** Max’s lines are the reading’s own words (quoted), in reading order; before Finish he invites and does not sign off; after Finish the closing comes once.
- **failure:** Deep’s six failure modes: templating, positional drift, reverse-as-opposite, parallel clarification, concatenation instead of synthesis, numerical/symbolic overreach.

- **The Devil** (ID 15; Major; number 15; element Earth; astrology Capricorn)
  - Upright keywords: Attachment, Awareness, Reclaiming Choice. Upright: A habit, agreement or desire may be narrowing your sense of choice. Look honestly at its rewards and costs, especially where shame keeps it hidden; begin with one boundary you can actually keep.
  - Reversed keywords: Release, Recognition, Breaking Patterns. Reversed: A binding pattern may be becoming easier to recognise or loosen. Support that awareness with practical changes and help where needed; insight alone does not have to carry the whole recovery.
- **The Hierophant** (ID 5; Major; number 5; element Earth; astrology Taurus)
  - Upright keywords: Tradition, Learning, Belonging. Upright: A teacher, tradition or shared practice can give your experience a useful framework. Learn the reasons behind the rules, then decide which values you want to carry into daily life.
  - Reversed keywords: Dogma, Conformity, Questioning. Reversed: Inherited rules may no longer fit your experience. Question a teaching without dismissing everything it offered; distinguish an honest search for meaning from obedience motivated by fear.
- **Three of Cups** (ID 38; Minor, Cups; number 3, Three; element Water; astrology Water · Cups)
  - Upright keywords: Friendship, Community, Celebration. Upright: Friendship and shared joy can nourish a situation that has become too solitary. Make room for mutual support and celebrate together without overlooking anyone doing unseen work.
  - Reversed keywords: Exclusion, Overindulgence, Social Strain. Reversed: A social group may feel excluding or gatherings may be leaving you depleted. Choose connections that allow honest participation rather than belonging at any cost.
- **Four of Cups** (ID 39; Minor, Cups; number 4, Four; element Water; astrology Water · Cups)
  - Upright keywords: Contemplation, Reassessment, Disengagement. Upright: A pause in interest may be asking you to examine what you actually want. Rest from automatic choices, while remaining willing to notice an offer that differs from your expectations.
  - Reversed keywords: Renewed Interest, Withdrawal, Missed Opening. Reversed: Interest may be returning, or withdrawal may be keeping a useful opening out of view. Re-engage gradually and test what feels nourishing rather than forcing enthusiasm.
- **Judgement** (ID 20; Major; number 20; element Fire; astrology Fire)
  - Upright keywords: Awakening, Review, Renewed Purpose. Upright: An honest review of the past can help you answer a new sense of purpose. Recognise what you have learned, make amends where possible, and choose the responsibility you are ready to accept now.
  - Reversed keywords: Self-Criticism, Avoidance, Unanswered Call. Reversed: Harsh self-criticism or fear of change may be drowning out a useful inner call. Distinguish responsibility from punishment and take one concrete step instead of waiting to feel fully worthy.
