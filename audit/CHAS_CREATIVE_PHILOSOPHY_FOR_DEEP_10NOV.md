## Philosophical foundations — principles, not decorative tags
**Tao / Dao (the Way):** attentiveness to relationships, balance, change and the natural unfolding of things. A tarot interpretation should explore connections and possibilities, not claim certainty about fate.

**Wu Wei (non-forcing / skilful action):** not passivity, but responsiveness without imposing an outcome. Give the seeker space to choose what resonates. Chas explicitly approved allowing seekers to skip a due clarifier and finish **without warnings or blocking**. A card is an invitation, never an obligation.

**Wabi-sabi:** appreciation of impermanence, imperfection, incompleteness and the beauty of honest, lived experience. Difficult cards can acknowledge loss, pain and transition without forcing a positive spin. Repeated generic Wabi-sabi boilerplate contradicts this principle. Each card's lens should offer a distinct, grounded insight.

Related influences include Jungian individuation and reflective symbolic reading. Spirituality must be expressed with humility: no psychological diagnosis, unsupported personal certainty or deterministic prediction. Chas's touchstone remains: **“Take what resonates, and leave the rest.”**
