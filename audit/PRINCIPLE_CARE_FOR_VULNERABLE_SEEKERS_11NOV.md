**Authority:** Chas, in conversation with Claude, 11 November 2026.

## What Chas said
> "people that come and seek this stuff are sometimes vulnerable … we have to look at the language that we are using
> [it is too] harsh, but that's for later"

## What it means
People often come to a reading at a hard moment: grief, a break-up, illness, money worry, loneliness, a decision they
are afraid of. The product must never make that worse. The cards can name difficulty honestly. The *way* we say it is
the part we control.

## Interim rule for new wording, from today
Anything newly written for Max, the reading page, the Golden Thread, the PDF, buttons or notes should:
1. **Name difficulty without verdict.** "This card can describe a hard stretch", not "you will suffer" or "this ends badly".
2. **Leave the future open.** It's a perspective, not a prediction (already house style: keep it).
3. **Hand choice back.** The seeker decides: "you may", "if it resonates", "take what resonates, and leave the rest".
4. **No alarm words used for effect:** doom, ruin, betrayal, disaster, punishment, curse, failure *as a label for the person*.
5. **No diagnosis or advice beyond our lane.** No medical, legal, financial or mental-health claims.
6. **Kind under pressure.** Read every line as if the person hearing it has just had the worst week of their year.
