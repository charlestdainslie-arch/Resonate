# Si Aye independent tarot research — source intake (10 Nov 2026)
Issue #51. Independently authored preliminary source assessment, **not** a completed ten-card comparison, prediction proof or product approval.

1. **V&A museum, history:** Tarot existed first as cards for games; later eighteenth-century divination and subsequent occult/kabbalistic layers developed over time. Avoid unverified ancient-Egypt-origin claims. https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/tarot-cards/
2. **Primary RWS source:** A.E. Waite, *Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1910/1911), is an important near-contemporaneous source for Waite's descriptions/meanings of Pamela Colman Smith's deck; it does not demonstrate empirical predictive accuracy. https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot
3. **Golden Dawn primary/documentary trail:** Identify exact manuscripts, editions and school before asserting tarot correspondence of planets, Hebrew letters, Qabalah or elements; avoid silently equating Hermetic Qabalah with Jewish Kabbalah. https://hermetic.com/gdlibrary/index
4. **Original artwork and colours:** V&A and Whitney museum accounts establish Pamela Colman Smith's substantial design contribution; an observed colour is not proof of a fixed esoteric meaning absent a source. https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/tarot-cards/ ; https://whitneymedia.org/assets/generic_file/1993/At_the_Dawn_Exhibition_Labels_6.1.22.pdf
5. **Kybalion dating:** Published in 1908 by 'Three Initiates', not a documented ancient-Egyptian tarot manual. Treat its philosophy as a modern optional lens. https://yogipublicationsocietyhistory.org/publications/the-kybalion-three-initiates/
6. **Modern Jungian tarot:** Sallie Nichols's *Jung and Tarot* published in 1980 (after Jung's death), so Nichols's tarot interpretations are not automatically Jung's direct teachings. https://ofj.org/library/jung-and-tarot-an-archetypal-journal/
7. **Licensing:** Rights for actual card images, modern recolourings, fonts and contemporary authors' wording must be checked before publishing or charging, not presumed from original deck date.

Evidence hierarchy: A contemporaneous primary text/art; B museum/academic source; C named later esoteric practitioner/school; D clearly attributed RESONATE original interpretive inference; X unsupported or falsely attributed. Preserve disagreements and unknowns.

Separate (a) correct registry/position/orientation/clarifier fidelity, (b) documented interpretive provenance, (c) ethical nondeterministic narrative, (d) unproven future predictions. Do not claim tarot predictive accuracy was scientifically verified.
