# RESONATE Clarifier UAT

1. Draw a spread containing one Major Arcana: exactly one clarifier should appear attached to that Major.
2. Draw a spread containing two or more Majors: each Major should receive its own unique clarifier.
3. Clarifiers must not replace or increase the number of spread positions.
4. A clarifier must never duplicate a card already in the spread or another clarifier.
5. Clarifier orientation may be upright or reversed.
6. Card artwork must display the complete Rider-Waite card using contain sizing; no cropping.
7. Reversed clarifier artwork rotates as a complete card.
8. Clarifier text identifies the Major it supports.
9. Production/main remains unchanged until UAT approval.
